## 1. What the run data showed (all confirmed against `runs (71).json`)

Full reasoning is in the session; the durable facts:

- **Watch died at 20.62 km (elapsed 6552 s).** One 15 s gap in the track; phone GPS took over
  distance (existing fallback) but the session clock (`watchElapsedSeconds`) froze → saved
  1:49:13 vs real 1:54:09, km-21 split recorded as 4:15, "Final 250m" projected a finish time
  earlier than the moment it was spoken. HR froze at 126 bpm into all 240 later points.
- **"Coach said 4:30, watch said 4:40"** — two different Garmin measurements. Km splits come
  from watch distance ÷ watch timer (what Garmin Connect's lap table shows); the wrist pace
  field is Doppler `currentSpeed`, which integrated ~2% low overall and 4–5% low over km 1–4.
  Android additionally overwrote the watch-speed pace with a position-derived one (fixed).
  Worth checking Garmin Connect's lap 1 for this activity — if it says ~4:37, the coach was right.
- **Target-pace nagging**: 2 h target = 5:41/km, ran ~4:50 → 15–21% ahead the whole way, and
  the HR / split / ETA prompts each re-raised "settle back to target" every time they fired.
- **"Approaching halfway" at 31%, "final stretch" at 45%** — positive-reinforcement cues with
  no run-stage rule in the prompt.
- **"Steep hill" on gentle rises**: the live grade was `Δ(5-sample altitude mean) ÷ one GPS
  step (~6 m)` — p5–p95 of ±8% on ground within ±5%; 421 ticks read ≥5% at a median true
  grade of 2.6%. All four "steep" callouts in the transcript were noise; the run's one real
  6–10% climb (km 7) was called "gentle". `steepestIncline` was 50.5% on a 22 m-range course.
- **"224 m of climbing"** was the accumulated ascent on a course whose altitude range is 22 m.
- **Wind** (34 km/h, mostly headwind) was captured and shown post-run but never reached a
  live coaching prompt.

## 2. Android + backend fixes (`9b73126`) — see `iOS_HALF_MARATHON_FIELD_REVIEW_2026-09-20_BRIEF.md` for the full contract

**Android `RunTrackingService.kt`**
- `currentWatchClockMs()` / `currentWatchClockSeconds()` — watch timer authoritative while
  frames arrive; after 15 s stale, carried forward on the phone's active clock. Feeds live
  timer, ETA, km-split clock, saved duration.
- `checkHeartRateStaleness()` on the 1 Hz tick — 30 s without an accepted sample → HR unknown.
- `onNewLocation()` no longer overwrites the watch's speed-derived pace for `garmin` fixes.
- `isInFinalStretch()` → `remaining <= 500` (past target counts; no post-finish "Struggle").
  **Side effect:** HR/cadence/analysis coaching now stays quiet after the target distance is
  passed, same as km splits already did. If runners routinely go well past a target, gate
  only the struggle detector instead.
- Pace/altitude series continue from phone GPS during the fallback (~2 s spacing).
- `trailingWindowGradePercent()` — grade over the trailing 100 m; steepest incline/decline
  tracked incrementally from it. `STEEP_UPHILL/DOWNHILL_GRADE_THRESHOLD` = ±10 (was 5 / −9);
  struggle hill tolerance from 6%.
- `currentWindContext()` / `currentElevationRangeM()` attached to split, HR, elite, struggle
  and elevation requests; HR also sends `kmSplits/distance/targetDistance/targetTime`.
- `RunSummaryScreen`: stat tile "Elevation" = max − min altitude; Max Incline in **%** (was °);
  difficulty factor uses the range. `RawDataViews`: wind is km/h (was labelled m/s ×3.6).

**Server**
- `ai-service.ts` shared helpers: `assessTargetPaceSettled` / `settledTargetPaceRule`
  (≥3 consistent ahead-of-target splits, adjacent ±15 s, OR past halfway with projected finish
  ≥5% inside target → target pace ruled out as a lever), `runStageRule`, `describeGrade` +
  `GRADE_VOCAB_RULE` (steep only ≥10%), `courseElevationLine` (range, not accumulation),
  `windLine`. Wired into split, HR, elite (status block, ETA, pace-trend), struggle, elevation
  and phase prompts. Replayed on the real splits: "ahead of target" allowed at km 1–2 only.
- Pre-run brief printed % grades with a `°` sign — fixed. Comprehensive-analysis label
  thresholds → ±10.
- `routes.ts`: HR route forwards the new fields; `km_splits`/`elevation_range_m` aliased on
  pace-update and elite routes (iOS sends snake_case — the server's split-based rules never
  saw iOS's splits before).
- Share image (`share-image-service.ts`): `elevationRangeFor()` — max − min from the row,
  else the GPS track's altitude spread, else gain. Label "Elevation".
- Consistent-splits praise (adjacent ±10 s) deliberately **not** tightened — Daniel's call.

## 3. Live-tracking fixes (observer side) — `9b73126` + iOS

1. **Invitation email had no code / dead button** (Jacinda, registered friend, Gmail on iOS).
   The friend template (`sendFriendLiveRunInvitationEmail`) never showed the code and its only
   button was `airuncoach://…`, which Gmail ignores. Now: both templates show the 8-char code
   and manual steps, buttons are `https://airuncoach.live/observe/{code}` — a new
   server-rendered landing page (`routes.ts`, next to `/api/observe/:code`) with the runner,
   status, code, "Open in AI Run Coach" (custom scheme works from a Safari tap), store links.
   `ensureSessionInviteCode()` guarantees a code before the friend email is sent.
   *Not done:* iOS Associated Domains / AASA so the https link opens the app directly — Android
   already has App Links for `/share/`; add `/observe/` to both when convenient.
2. **"Last update 3m 21s ago — may have lost signal" with full signal** (Chris, iOS). The
   observer compared the server's `lastSyncedAt` to its own clock; iOS suspends the poll loop
   when the phone is locked, so on unlock the age was simply how long *his* phone was locked.
   Both observer screens now measure age on the observer's clock from when `lastSyncedAt` last
   advanced, only while their own polling is alive (fetch within 10 s); iOS re-polls
   immediately on `scenePhase == .active`.
3. **Observer clock stepped in 5 s lumps** → ticks locally between syncs (`reconcileElapsed`
   on both VMs; re-anchors only if the server is ahead of the prediction or >3 s behind it;
   stops ticking when paused/ended). Distance and pace still update on the 5 s sync.
