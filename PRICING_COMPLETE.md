- **Non-US conversion**: +15-25% from currency localization

1. **Psychological pricing**: $5.99 feels cheaper than $7.99 (not just math)

3. **Trust signal**: App knows where they are = professional
4. **Lower barrier**: $5.99/mo is easier to commit to than $7.99
5. **Annual incentive**: Discount pushes commitment from monthly
