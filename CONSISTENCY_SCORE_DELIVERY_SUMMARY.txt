───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE FORMULA
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Consistency Score = (1 - CV × 5) × 100%

Where CV = Coefficient of Variation = Standard Deviation / Mean

Clamped to [0%, 100%]

Data Sources (in priority):
  1. km split times (if ≥ 2 splits available)
  2. pace data samples (if ≥ 10 samples available)
  3. Show "—" if neither available
